## en-US en-GB en-AU en-CA en-IN en-SG en-ZA
Nome: Structural Mechanics
Breve: Solve structural exercises from a photo or drawing, with steps and theory
Completa:
SdC is the app for studying Structural Mechanics: start from a photo or a drawing of the structure and get the solution with a step-by-step worked solution and the theory behind each step.

WHAT IT DOES
• Photograph the exercise or draw the structure with the built-in editor, on a grid sheet
• The solution appears at once in a pull-up panel; from there open the full worked solution
• Kinematic analysis: degrees of freedom and constraints, and whether the structure is statically determinate, a mechanism or statically indeterminate
• Support reactions and forces in internal hinges, also with the graphical method
• Pressure line and diagrams of axial force, shear and bending moment
• Kinematic chains and the principle of virtual work to find reaction components
• Results in symbolic form (for example 3/8 ql) and in numbers when you enter the data
• Share the worked solution as text with other apps, to send it to a colleague or save it
• History of your exercises always at hand

MADE FOR STUDYING
Everything on a single sheet: scroll, zoom and follow the reasoning one step at a time. Every step has its "why", to understand the method and check your own calculations while you prepare.
